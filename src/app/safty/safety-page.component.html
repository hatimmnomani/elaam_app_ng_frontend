Child Safety Standards Policy

Effective Date: 20 Feb 2025

Version: 1.0

Approved By: R.K. Bajpai

1. Purpose

The purpose of this Policy is to establish clear, legally compliant, and enforceable standards for the protection of children using Aelaam's services. This Policy affirms our zero-tolerance stance on child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and outlines the mechanisms for compliance with applicable local, national, and international laws.

2. Scope

This Policy applies to:

  • All users of Aelaam services, including children, guardians, and general users.
  • All employees, contractors, and third-party service providers engaged by Aelaam.
  • All digital services, mobile applications, websites, and related systems operated under the Aelaam brand.

3. Legal Frameworks and Applicable Laws

Aelaam complies with globally recognized child protection regulations, including but not limited to:

  • United Nations Convention on the Rights of the Child (UNCRC), 1989
  • Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), U.S.
  • EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) – Articles 6, 8, and relevant Recitals regarding children's data
  • UK Data Protection Act 2018 and Age-Appropriate Design Code
  • Convention on Cybercrime (Budapest Convention), Council of Europe
  • Applicable local and national child protection and online safety laws in all jurisdictions where the service operates.

4. Definitions

  • Child: Any individual under the age of 18, as per UNCRC Article 1, unless local laws specify a younger age for majority.
  • CSAE: Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation, including any imagery, grooming behaviour, sexual solicitation, trafficking, or endangerment of a child online.
  • Personal Data: As defined under GDPR and equivalent laws, including names, addresses, geolocation, online identifiers, behavioural data, and multimedia content.

5. Policy Principles

5.1 Zero Tolerance for CSAE

  • Any CSAE-related conduct, imagery, or communication is strictly prohibited.
  • Immediate removal of prohibited content and permanent suspension or banning of responsible users will be enforced.
  • Mandatory reporting obligations will be executed through cooperation with national law enforcement agencies, the National Centre for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC), INTERPOL, and similar authorities.

5.2 Reporting and Redressal

  • A dedicated in-app reporting mechanism shall be provided for users to report CSAE, grooming, or inappropriate conduct.
  • Reports are reviewed within [Insert SLA timeframe, e.g., 24 hours], and urgent cases are prioritized.
  • Affected accounts are reviewed by trained moderators and escalated to child protection officers.

5.3 Data Collection and Privacy

  • No personal data from children under 13 shall be collected without verifiable parental consent, in compliance with COPPA and GDPR Article 8.
  • Minimal and purpose-specific data collection for children (e.g., for account creation or parental monitoring) will follow data minimization and storage limitation principles.
  • Appropriate technical and organizational measures (encryption, restricted access, audits) are implemented to safeguard children's personal data.

5.4 Age-Appropriate Design and Access Controls

  • Age-verification mechanisms shall be implemented where required.
  • Access to app features and content will be restricted based on age-appropriate categorization, with additional parental control functionality provided.

6. Enforcement and Accountability

  • Violations of this Policy by users will result in immediate suspension, permanent account termination, and reporting to authorities.
  • Employees or contractors found in breach of this Policy are subject to disciplinary action, termination, and where applicable, referral to law enforcement.
  • Cooperation with global child protection agencies shall be maintained to ensure proactive and reactive measures against CSAE.

7. Monitoring, Audit, and Review

  • Regular internal audits and external compliance reviews will assess adherence to laws and safety standards.
  • Automated detection tools (e.g., hash-matching databases such as PhotoDNA) will be used to identify prohibited materials.
  • This Policy shall be reviewed annually or upon regulatory change, whichever occurs earlier, and updated as needed to reflect legal or platform requirements.
